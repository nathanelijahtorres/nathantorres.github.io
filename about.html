About Me

I am Nathan Elijah Torres, a System Administrator passionate about building and maintaining infrastructure that is stable, secure, and efficient. I enjoy solving operational challenges, improving system performance, and finding practical ways to automate repetitive tasks and streamline day-to-day operations.

As I continue to expand my knowledge beyond traditional systems administration, I have developed a growing interest in cloud technologies and AI-driven solutions. I am an AWS Certified AI Practitioner, and I see this achievement as an important milestone in my ongoing journey toward building more scalable, intelligent, and efficient systems.

My career is now moving toward CloudOps and DevOps, where I aim to combine my systems administration experience with cloud platforms, CI/CD, infrastructure as code, and observability. I am driven by the goal of helping teams deliver reliable solutions faster while maintaining security, resilience, and operational excellence.

I believe that continuous learning, thoughtful automation, and a strong foundation in infrastructure are key to building better systems—and I look forward to applying these principles throughout my career.
