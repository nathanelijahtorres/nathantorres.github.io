Setting up a Self-Hosted Vaultwarden at Home

Self-Hosting HomeLab Docker Vaultwarden Security

I wanted a secure password manager that I fully control, so I deployed Vaultwarden in my homelab environment. This gave me ownership of my data while keeping the Bitwarden-compatible experience across devices.

I run Vaultwarden as a Docker container, which makes deployment, updates, and rollback workflows cleaner. I paired it with persistent storage and regular backup routines to protect vault data in case of host issues.

Hosting it at home also gave me practical experience in securing exposed services: HTTPS, controlled access, and operational monitoring. It is now one of the most valuable services in my HomeLab and a strong step in my self-hosting journey.
